## Two points worth knowing when the response comes back

1. **Note 9 and the Fig. 40 data sheet do not agree on the load path.** Note 9
   says the riser rests on steel. The data sheet says load is carried by shear
   lugs welded to the pipe. Without lugs the Fig. 40 relies on bolt friction,
   which the manufacturer does not rate. This is the substance of the shear lug
   question and is why it is raised as a conflict rather than an omission.
2. **The Fig. 261 data sheet carries its own caveats.** Service: "This product is
   not intended for use with hanger rods. For this application refer to Fig. 40
   Riser Clamp." That caveat is about rod hangers, not steel-borne risers, so it
   does not rule out the substitution here. But Installation adds: "Clamp is
   fitted and bolted preferably below a coupling, hub or welded lugs on steel
   pipe." The Fig. 261 therefore also prefers a positive stop. If Engineering
   accepts the substitution on load alone, the load path question stays open.
   Not raised in the RFI, since the recommended solution already asks whether
   shear lugs are required.
