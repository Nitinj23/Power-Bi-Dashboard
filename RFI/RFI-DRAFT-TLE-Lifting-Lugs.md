## 5. Tecsar Position / Impact

Tecsar wishes to draw NOVA's attention to the following, which bear directly on the response:

1. **Note 10 is a hard constraint.** The EA2102D-049 lugs were designed for a single configuration — EA-2102D channel installed on the EA-2107B TLE tube section. The note expressly states that any future change must be re-evaluated by engineering. The existing design therefore **cannot simply be replicated** onto other TLE covers without a new engineering assessment.

2. **The lugs are rated for in-plane loading only** (Note 7: 21521 lb in-plane). Any lift introducing an out-of-plane or angled sling component falls outside the qualified design.

3. **Installation is not a field-only activity.** Notes 4, 5, 8 and 9 require preheat to 400°F, PWHT at 1250°F for 5 hr 45 min, PT examination of first and final weld passes, and a 91 PSIG hydrotest of the channel after welding. Retrofitting lugs to additional covers carries significant cost, schedule and shop/field-logistics implications, and engages TSSA / CSA B51 registration requirements per Note 2.

4. Accordingly, if the response to Question 2 or 3 above is affirmative, Tecsar considers this a **change in scope** and will submit a change notice with cost and schedule impact.
