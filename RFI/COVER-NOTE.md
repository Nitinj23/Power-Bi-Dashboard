## The three conflicts raised

1. Section 6.1.9 states lugs were installed on EA-2107A & B in 2022. Drawing
   Note 10 records one installation only: EA-2102D channel on the EA-2107B tube
   section. EA-2107A is unaccounted for.
2. Section 6.1.9 contemplates moving a cover onto EA-2107C. Note 10 restricts the
   design to the EA-2102D/EA-2107B configuration and requires engineering
   re-evaluation for any future change.
3. Section 6.1.9 calls 43,042 lbs a "combined vertical lifting capacity". That is
   2 x 21,521 lb, and Note 7 rates each lug 21,521 lb IN-PLANE ONLY.
