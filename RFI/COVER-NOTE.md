Section 6.1.9 text now inserted verbatim; no placeholders remain.

## What 6.1.9 actually requires

It directs the contractor to use the EXISTING cover lifting lugs to lift
EA-2107A, B and C, citing drawing EA2102D-049. It does not ask for new lugs.
The RFI was re-framed accordingly.

## The three conflicts raised

1. Section 6.1.9 states lugs were installed on EA-2107A & B in 2022. Drawing
   EA2102D-049 Rev 0.2 Note 10 records one installation only: the EA-2102D
   channel on the EA-2107B tube section. EA-2107A is unaccounted for, and field
   review found no lugged covers at all.
2. Section 6.1.9 contemplates moving a cover onto EA-2107C. Note 10 restricts
   the design to the EA-2102D/EA-2107B configuration and requires engineering
   re-evaluation for any future change.
3. Section 6.1.9 calls 43,042 lbs a "combined vertical lifting capacity". That
   figure is 2 x 21,521 lb, and drawing Note 7 rates each lug 21,521 lb
   IN-PLANE ONLY. A vertical two-lug lift with angled slings does not load the
   lugs in-plane. Sling arrangement needs confirming.

## Not raised in the RFI

Section 6.1.9 also requires Magnetic Particle Testing of all existing lifting
lug welds prior to each lift, with a report to NOVA. If the lugs do not exist,
that obligation cannot be discharged. It follows from the answer to question 1
and was left out to keep the RFI to one page.
